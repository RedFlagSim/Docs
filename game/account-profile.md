# Account & Profile

Your Red Flag Sim profile is the identity used for progress, logbook records, rankings, qualifications, and medals.

## Account Types

You can start quickly with an anonymous account. This is useful for trying the game, but it is tied to the current saved account state.

For long-term play, link or sign in with a Unity ID. A linked account is the safer choice if you reinstall, change devices, or want your progress to remain associated with the same identity.

## What Your Profile Contains

Your profile can include:

- pilot display name;
- callsign;
- squadron or flight information;
- country;
- rank and sublevel;
- Career XP;
- total flight time;
- login streak and activity history used for progression.

Leaderboards and public displays use a smaller public version of your profile, such as display name, callsign, avatar, rank, sublevel, and XP.

## Keeping Progress Safe

Progress belongs to the signed-in player account. If you switch accounts, the game switches to that account's profile, records, qualifications, and medals.

If you play anonymously, consider linking the account before relying on long-term progress. Linking helps preserve your pilot identity and makes it easier to recover progress later.

## When Profile Data Updates

Your profile can update after:

- signing in;
- opening the logbook;
- completing flights or skill events;
- receiving Career XP;
- earning or renewing a qualification;
- unlocking a medal.
