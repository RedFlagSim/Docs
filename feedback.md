# User Feedback

We value your suggestions and feedback. If you encounter any bugs during gameplay, have suggestions for improvement, or have anything else you'd like to tell us, please feel free to contact us via email.

::: tip Note
Direct email feedback is currently the most effective way to communicate. We carefully read every message and continuously optimize the game experience in subsequent updates.
:::

## How to Provide Feedback

You can send an email directly to our developer email address:

<div class="feedback-card">
  <p><strong>Developer Email:</strong></p>
  <p><a href="mailto:support@redflagsim.com?subject=Red%20Flag%20Sim%20Feedback" class="email-link">support@redflagsim.com</a></p>
</div>

### Suggested Information
To help us locate issues or understand your suggestions faster, we recommend including the following in your email:
*   **Feedback Type**: Bug Report / Feature Suggestion / Other
*   **Device Model**: e.g., iPhone 15 Pro, iPad Pro (M2)
*   **iOS Version**: e.g., iOS 17.4
*   **Detailed Description**: Steps to reproduce the issue or details of your suggestion
*   **Screenshots/Videos**: If you have relevant visual materials, please send them as attachments
