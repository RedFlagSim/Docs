# The Air Superiority Benchmark

![F-15C Header](/assets/F-15C/header.jpg)

Step into the cockpit of the most successful air superiority fighter in history. In Red Flag Sim, the F-15C is not just the first playable module—it is the technical benchmark for everything that follows. You are not just piloting a 3D model; you are managing a complex organism of aerodynamics, sensors, and combat logic.

## Aerodynamics: Physics Over Scripts

You don't just "turn" an Eagle; you manage energy, lift, and drag. Fitted to within 5% of real wind-tunnel data, the flight model ensures that every maneuver is a response to the physical atmosphere. When you pull Gs, you are struggling with physics, not a preset animation.

## Fire Control Radar: See First, Kill First

The radar screen is not a "magic dot" generator. Modeled on real radar equations, the AN/APG-63 is a physical sensor you must master. To own the sky, you must understand RCS, ground clutter, and closure rates. Target management and track fusion are implemented with industrial-grade precision to give you a true pilot's situational awareness.

## Avionics: Your Office at 30,000 Feet

HUD, VSD, RWR, and MPCD are recreated with near-100% fidelity to real technical documentation. From font sizes to symbol spacing, every pixel serves the pilot's workflow. The display logic is designed to let you process combat data as a real Eagle driver would, ensuring your decisions are based on authentic information.

## Systems: A Living Machine

Under the skin, the F-15C is a complex web of hydraulics, electrical buses, and fuel systems. As fuel burns and weapons are released, you will feel the shift in center of gravity and handling. Managing these systems is as critical as mastering the stick and rudder.

## Combat: Beyond the Range Ring

Most games tell you when a target is "in range." In Red Flag Sim, you'll learn that range is just one factor. Altitude, speed, aspect angle, and closure rate determine if your AIM-120 or AIM-9 actually has the energy to reach its mark. Every launch is a calculated decision, not a button press.

## Growth: Qualify on the Eagle

Mastering the F-15C is your path to becoming a certified combat pilot. From basic flight maneuvers to BVR (Beyond Visual Range) engagements, your performance is tracked. Earn your F-15C qualification and keep your status Current in the Red Flag Sim pilot registry.
