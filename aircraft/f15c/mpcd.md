# MPCD

The Multi-Purpose Color Display (MPCD) is the central weapon status display in the F-15C.

This page will document how MPCD, HUD, VSD and Touch UI are coordinated, including weapon load status, weapon-control context and selected-target handoff indicators.

- In some operating modes, `castle` right on the VSD/SIT context switches to loadout/armament management on MPCD.
- Use MPCD together with HUD and VSD data; MPCD values are supportive context and do not replace all launch conditions checks.
