# The Dual-Role Strike Master

![F-15E Header](/assets/title-placeholder.svg)

The F-15E Strike Eagle is more than just a bomber; it is an all-weather, multi-role weapon system. In Red Flag Sim, the "Mudhen" introduces a new layer of complexity: the seamless integration of precision ground-attack sensors with air-to-air dominance.

## The Power of Two

Success in the Strike Eagle is built on coordination. Manage the mission from the front or back seat, balancing radar management, targeting pod logic, and weapon delivery. In high-threat environments, your ability to multitask and communicate is as critical as your flight skills.

## Own the Night and the Clouds

The F-15E's advanced sensors aren't just tools; they are your eyes in the dark. Master the Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) and LANTIRN systems to generate ground maps and identify targets when the eyes of others fail. Every strike is a result of sensor mastery and calculated penetration.

## Coming Soon

The F-15E module is currently under development. It will follow the same technical baseline as the F-15C, featuring high-fidelity aerodynamics, coupled systems simulation, and pixel-perfect cockpit avionics. Stay tuned for the first flight of the Mudhen.
