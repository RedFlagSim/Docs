# About Red Flag Sim

## Serious Aviation, Within Reach

Most air combat games tell you that flying is easy. Red Flag Sim tells you that flying is **worth it**.

We believe that simulation depth shouldn't be a barrier to entry, but a destination for those who want to master the sky. Red Flag Sim is a hardcore modern fighter simulator built for Mobile and VR, where military-grade physics meet a redesigned, intuitive interaction layer. You are not just piloting a model; you are managing a fighter.

---

## What Makes It Real?

**Physics Over Presets:**
Every maneuver is a response to the atmosphere. Our flight models are fitted to within 5% of real wind-tunnel data, incorporating dynamic center-of-gravity and inertia. When the aircraft responds, it's the physics talking, not a script.

**Authentic Pilot Workflow:**
You won't find simplified "arcade" controls here. We've restored real flight-control laws and stability augmentation logic. From radar mode management to engine RPM response, every system follows the same functional logic as its real-world counterpart.

**Deep System Coupling:**
A fighter is a living organism. In Red Flag Sim, the propulsion, hydraulics, electrical, and navigation systems are all independently modeled and real-time coupled. As fuel burns and weapons are released, your aircraft's behavior changes—demanding constant pilot awareness and decision-making.

---

## The Path to Mastery: Learn. Qualify. Stay Current.

Red Flag Sim is more than a one-off mission; it is a journey of growth. Through our structured pilot training and certification system, you will progress from basic flight maneuvers to complex carrier recoveries and aerial refueling.

Every successful qualification is recorded in your pilot file. But a pilot's skill is only as good as their last flight—Red Flag Sim tracks your "Current" status, encouraging you to stay sharp and maintain your edge.

---

## Development Roadmap

Our mission is to bring serious military aviation simulation to more platforms while maintaining its core depth. Our current milestones include:
- **Global Map System:** A world built for global-scale operations.
- **Aircraft Expansion:** More legendary airframes, each modeled to the same technical baseline.
- **Multiplayer Combat:** Cross-platform matchmaking for the ultimate test of skill.
- **Full Platform Support:** Bringing the Eagle to PC, Mac, and consoles.
